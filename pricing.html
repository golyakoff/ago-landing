What is priced, per unit

Two published prices, and one thing that is real in the product but has no published price yet.

Item Price What it covers
Base price Covers the account and its first 3 operator seats.
Each operator seat past the third Charged once per seat, up to 5 seats in total.
Each administrator past the second A real thing you can buy inside the product — the published price for it is not out yet, so this page will not print one. Ask us and we will tell you.
Each connected channel beyond the website widget One flat price for any channel — Telegram, WhatsApp, SMS and others, arriving one at a time in the same operator console. The published price for it is not out yet, so this page will not print one. Ask us and we will tell you.
